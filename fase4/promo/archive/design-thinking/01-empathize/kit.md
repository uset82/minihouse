## 1 · Questionnaire for Carlos

**Purpose:** get the owner's goals, constraints and taste on paper before the interviews, so the interviews test the right hypotheses. Written, not spoken: Carlos can answer in Spanish, English or Norwegian, in his own words, at any time.

**How to return it:** copy this section into `fase4/promo/01-empathize/carlos-interview.md` and answer under each question. That file is **git-ignored and stays on this PC**: the GitHub repo is public, and the answers cover budget and price ambition. It is never committed; the synthesis quotes only what is needed, without figures that shouldn't be public. Short answers are fine; «vet ikke» is a valid answer and better than a guess.

### 1.1 Formål / Purpose

1. Hvorfor vil du promotere MiniHouse nå? Hva skal nettsiden oppnå det neste halvåret? / *Why promote MiniHouse now, and what should the site achieve in the next six months?*
2. Hvem ser du for deg at skal bo i huset? Beskriv én person så konkret du kan. / *Who do you picture living in it? Describe one person as concretely as you can.*
3. Hvem tror du dette huset er for? Beskriv én person så konkret du kan. / *Who do you picture this house for? Describe one person as concretely as you can.*
4. Hvilke tre situasjoner skal nettsiden få folk til å ta kontakt i? / *Which three situations should make someone get in touch?*

### 1.2 Penger, tid og marked

5. Hva er byggebudsjettet for prototypen, og hva er prisantydningen du ser for deg for et ferdig hus? / *What is the build budget for the prototype, and what price ambition do you have for a finished house?*
6. Når regner du med at det første huset kan vises fram fysisk? / *When do you expect the first house can be shown in person?*
7. Hvor mange hus vil du kunne bygge i året, og hvem bygger dem? / *How many houses a year could you build, and who builds them?*
8. Er kundene privatpersoner, kommuner, utleiere eller andre? Hvem er den viktigste? / *Are customers private buyers, municipalities, landlords or others? Which is the main one?*
9. Er det greit at nettsiden sier at huset ikke er bygget ennå? Skal den si noe om når? / *Is it fine for the site to say the house is not built yet? Should it say when?*

### 1.3 Materialer (this feeds the material passport)

10. Hvor kommer det gjenbrukte treet fra i dag, og hvor mye har du? Hvilke treslag? / *Where does the reclaimed timber come from today, how much is there, and which species?*
11. Er det greit å skrive «malmfuru» og «eik» når partiet ikke er dokumentert, eller skal det stå «planlagt»? / *Is it acceptable to write "malmfuru" and "oak" before the lot is documented, or should it read "planned"?*
12. Har noe av treet vært behandlet (maling, tjære, trykkimpregnering)? / *Has any of the timber been treated (paint, tar, pressure treatment)?*
13. Har du bilder av de faktiske bordene, også endeved, som kan brukes? / *Do you have photos of the actual boards, end grain included, that we may use?*
14. Hvorfor akkurat kobber? Skal det være i badet, i detaljer eller begge steder? / *Why copper? Should it be in the bathroom, in details, or both?*

### 1.4 Merkevare og navn

15. Eier du navnet «MiniHouse», og er det registrert noe sted? / *Do you own the name "MiniHouse", and is it registered anywhere?*
16. Hvem skal stå som avsender og kontakt på nettsiden? E-post, telefon, firmanavn? / *Who is the sender and contact on the site? E-mail, phone, company name?*
17. Hvilke selskaper eller samarbeidspartnere skal ikke nevnes? / *Which companies or partners must not be mentioned?*

### 1.5 «Moderne retro norsk» (taste, in his own words)

18. Vis 15–20 referansebilder og sorter dem i «liker» og «liker ikke». Skriv én setning om hvorfor for hver haug. / *Sort 15–20 reference images into like and dislike, and write one sentence about why for each pile.*
19. Hvilke tre ting må være med på nettsiden, uansett? Og hva må aldri være med? / *Name three things that must be on the site, and three that must never be.*

### 1.6 Decisions still open (from [decisions.md](../../../decisions.md))

20. D6: hvilken innboks skal følge-skjemaet sende til, og hvilken tjeneste vil du bruke? / *Which inbox should the follow form send to, and which form service?*
21. Er det greit å bruke selve sidefoten til å si at huset er under utvikling, med dato? / *Is it fine for the footer to say the house is under development, with a date?*

## 3 · 30-minute interview guide

Same guide in both languages: Norwegian for the session, English so Cursor and Antigravity can read what was actually asked. Read the Norwegian aloud; the wording is written to be spoken, not printed.

**Before the call (2 min):** one participant, one file. Open the [note template](#8--note-template) and fill the header. Read the [consent wording](#7--consent-wording) first. Ask if they mind being recorded; the default is **no recording**, notes only.

| Min | Section | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Consent and framing | Make it safe, say what happens with the notes |
| 3–8 | Warm-up: last time they looked | Get a real story, not an opinion |
| 8–15 | Trust, and what makes them leave | Which signals decide for them |
| 15–22 | Reclaimed wood | Is old timber charm, quality or risk |
| 22–27 | Fears and objections | Damp, rot, permits, transport, price |
| 27–30 | What would make them get in touch | The trigger, in their words |

### 3.1 Consent and framing (0–3 min)

> **NO:** «Takk at du stiller. Jeg gjør dette for å lære hvordan folk tenker om små hus, ikke for å selge. Jeg noterer med en deltakerkode, ikke navn eller initialer, og notatene ligger bare hos meg. Du kan trekke deg når som helst, og du trenger ikke svare på alt. Er det greit at jeg noterer?»
> **EN:** "Thanks for doing this. I'm doing this to learn how people think about small houses, not to sell. I take notes with a participant code, not your name or initials, and the notes stay with me. You can stop at any time, and you don't have to answer everything. Is it okay if I take notes?"

### 3.2 Warm-up: the last time they looked (3–8 min)

> **NO:** «Fortell om sist du så på et lite hus, et tilbygg eller en hytte — hva var situasjonen?»
> **EN:** "Tell me about the last time you looked at a small house, an annex or a cabin — what was the situation?"
>
> Probes: «Hva søkte du på?» · «Hva fant du?» · «Hva gjorde du etterpå?» · «Hvor stoppet det?»
> Probes (EN): "What did you search for?" · "What did you find?" · "What did you do next?" · "Where did it stop?"

Open with behaviour, never with «ville du kjøpt et modulhus?». The second question is the one people answer politely.

### 3.3 Trust, and what makes them leave (8–15 min)

> **NO:** «Når du ser på en slik nettside eller annonse: hva gjør at du tror på den?»
> **EN:** "When you look at a site or a listing like that: what makes you believe it?"
>
> **NO:** «Hva gjør at du lukker fanen?»
> **EN:** "What makes you close the tab?"
>
> Probes: «Hva ville du sett etter først?» · «Hvilke bilder stoler du på?» · «Hva må stå der for at du skal tro at det er ekte?»

### 3.4 Reclaimed wood (15–22 min)

> **NO:** «Når du hører «gjenbrukt tre» i et hus, hva tenker du da?»
> **EN:** "When you hear 'reclaimed timber' in a house, what do you think?"
>
> Probes: «Er det mest sjarm eller mest risiko?» · «Hva ville du spurt om?» · «Hva må dokumenteres for at du skal stole på det?» · «Spiller treslaget noen rolle?»

This section decides whether the material passport reads as proof or as decoration. Write the answer in their words, not yours.

### 3.5 Fears and objections (22–27 min)

> **NO:** «Hva er det du er redd for kunne gå galt med et hus som dette?»
> **EN:** "What are you afraid could go wrong with a house like this?"
>
> Probes, one at a time, only if they don't come up: fukt og råte · byggesøknad og kommune · frakt og løft · pris og skjulte kostnader · oppvarming og strøm · forsikring · tomt og fundament · hvem som bygger det.
> Probes (EN): damp and rot · building permit and municipality · transport and lifting · price and hidden costs · heating and power · insurance · plot and foundation · who builds it.

Don't defend the house. If they say «dette virker billig og dårlig», write it down; that sentence is worth more than a compliment.

### 3.6 What would make them get in touch (27–30 min)

> **NO:** «Hva må være sant for at du skulle sendt en melding i morgen?»
> **EN:** "What would have to be true for you to send a message tomorrow?"
>
> **NO:** «Hvis du skulle beskrevet dette huset for en venn, hva ville du sagt?»
> **EN:** "If you described this house to a friend, what would you say?"

The second answer is the raw material for the hero lead and the FINN title. Keep the exact words.

### 3.7 Closing

> **NO:** «Er det noe jeg burde spurt om og ikke gjorde?»
> **EN:** "Is there anything I should have asked and didn't?"
>
> Then: thank them, say when they can see the sketches, and remind them they can ask to have their notes deleted.

## 5 · 5-second and preference test scripts (for Antigravity's G3 pack)

Antigravity builds the test pack (PLAN 3.2–3.3, G3). These scripts make the test repeatable and keep it honest. Antigravity shows the images; DeepSeek writes the questions; Carlos or Antigravity runs the session.

### 5.1 Five-second test of the hero

**Setup:** the hero at 360 px in a browser or as a screenshot, one direction at a time. Show for exactly 5 seconds, then hide it and ask. Do not let the participant scroll or zoom.

**Say:**
> **NO:** «Du får se et bilde i fem sekunder. Etterpå skjuler jeg det og stiller tre spørsmål. Se så mye du klarer.»
> **EN:** "You'll see an image for five seconds. Then I'll hide it and ask three questions. Look as much as you can."

**Show for 5 s, hide, then ask, in this order:**

1. «Hva var dette?» / "What was this?" — record the words.
2. «Var det noe du kunne kjøpt i dag?» / "Was it something you could buy today?" — yes / no / don't know, plus why.
3. «Hvem tror du det er for?» / "Who do you think it's for?"

**Pass criteria (PLAN 5.2):** at least 4 of 5 say it is a small movable house, and at least 4 of 5 notice that it is a concept rather than something they can buy today. A hero that hides the status fails even if everyone likes it.

**Record:** participant code (P1–P5), direction (A/B/C), the three answers verbatim, anything they noticed first.

### 5.2 Preference test of the three directions

**Setup:** the three style tiles, one page each, same hero and same material block. Show all three at the same size, at 1280 px and at 360 px. Rotate the order per participant (A-B-C, B-C-A, C-A-B) so position doesn't win.

**Say:**
> **NO:** «Her er tre retninger for samme hus. Jeg vil at du ser på dem og sier hvilken som føles mest som et norsk hus laget av gammelt tre. Det finnes ikke noe riktig svar.»
> **EN:** "Here are three directions for the same house. Look at them and tell me which feels most like a Norwegian house made of old wood. There is no right answer."

**Ask, in this order:**

1. «Hvilken liker du best? Hvorfor akkurat den?» / "Which do you like best, and why that one?"
2. «Hvilken ville du stolt på hvis du skulle kjøpe?» / "Which would you trust if you were buying?"
3. «Hvilken ville du gått fra?» / "Which would you walk away from?"
4. «Er det noe fra de andre du ville tatt med?» / "Is there anything from the others you'd take with you?"

**Pass criteria:** a direction is usable when it is **ranked first by at least 2 of 5**, and **walked away from by at most 1**. Record the rank, the reason in the participant's words, and any single element they name (a colour, a font, the material passport). If no direction clears both lines, that is a finding for G3 — report it rather than picking the least-bad tile.

## 6 · Usability script (G4/G5 round)

**Setup:** two options, both arranged by Cursor and both approved by Carlos first (protocol §5). A participant's phone cannot open `localhost:4322` — that address only resolves on the PC itself.

- **A · Same Wi-Fi (default).** Carlos's phone or the participant's phone joins the same network as the PC, and Cursor starts the preview with `npm run preview -- --port 4322 --host`. Carlos uses the LAN address Vite prints, for example `http://192.168.1.42:4322`, on the phone. Nothing is public; the session is in the same room or on a call where Carlos shares his screen.
- **B · Preview deploy (only with Carlos's OK).** For a remote participant on another network, Cursor uploads a preview build to a Canner URL and Carlos approves the deploy. The URL is public, so the preview holds nothing but the site: no participant data, no test submissions with real names.

Never type the participant's name or e-mail into a form during a test; use an obvious test value like «Test Testesen» and a disposable address Carlos controls.

**Think aloud, no coaching.** One participant at a time, 20–25 minutes.

**Opening:**
> **NO:** «Jeg tester nettsiden, ikke deg. Tenk høyt mens du gjør oppgavene. Jeg kommer ikke til å hjelpe deg med mindre du står fast, og da sier jeg bare «hva ville du gjort nå?».»
> **EN:** "I'm testing the site, not you. Think out loud as you do the tasks. I won't help unless you're stuck, and then I'll only ask 'what would you do now?'."

**The five tasks (from PLAN §10, 5.1):**

| # | Task, Norwegian | Task, English | Done when | Success criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | «Finn ut hva huset er laget av.» | "Find out what the house is made of." | The participant names steel and reclaimed timber | Names both, within 90 s, without opening the FAQ |
| T2 | «Finn ut om du kan kjøpe et hus i dag.» | "Find out whether you can buy one today." | Says it is not built yet / under development | Says "not yet" or "under development", not "yes"; must not invent a price or a delivery date |
| T3 | «Se på badet.» | "Take a look at the bathroom." | Opens the gallery or the living section and finds a bathroom image | Reaches a bathroom image within 60 s |
| T4 | «Finn ut om huset trenger byggesøknad.» | "Find out whether the house needs a building permit." | Finds the FAQ answer | Reads «det avhenger av bruken» or equivalent; does not conclude "no permit needed" |
| T5 | «Følg prosjektet.» | "Follow the project." | Reaches the form, types a test e-mail address, ticks the consent box and signs up (test sign-up only, clearly marked) | Finds the form, understands that only e-mail and an optional name are asked, no error they can't fix, and the confirmation reads «Takk! Du følger nå prosjektet.» |

**Observation rules:**

- Record **completed / partly / failed**, the time, and the exact words they use. Don't record opinions about colour or taste as usability findings.
- Every time they say «hvor er …», that is a finding about navigation, not about them.
- After T5, ask: «Hva ville du forventet skjedde nå?» / "What would you expect to happen now?"
- If they stop at the consent text, ask them to read it aloud and say what they think it means. That is the privacy copy's only real test.

**Pass criteria (PLAN 5.1):** at least 4 of 5 complete each task. Anything below that becomes a ranked finding in `05-test/usability.md` with an owner (Cursor for code, Antigravity for the visual cause, DeepSeek for the words).

**Record table:**

| Participant | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 |  |  |  |  |  |  |

Use ✅ done · ◐ partly · ✗ failed, plus seconds.
