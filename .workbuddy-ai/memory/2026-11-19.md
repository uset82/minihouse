- Native MCP endpoint `127.0.0.1:27182/mcp` is dead. Live path: FluidBridge/2.0 at

  one `ADVANCED_BREP_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` listing every solid** — the form Fusion's own
  exporter uses.
