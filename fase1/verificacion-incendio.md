**Qualifying escape windows: 1** (V-01).

Doors are not escape windows. They are checked only against the §11-14 free width:

| Door | Leaf mm | Free width required mm | Margin mm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P-01 | 900 | 860 | +40 |

A 900 mm leaf leaves only 40 mm for frame, hinge and stop build-up before the 0.86 m free width is lost. Confirm the free width on the door schedule; do not assume the nominal leaf size satisfies it.

## 4. What this screen does not settle

1. Whether the roof belongs to the main load-bearing system (R 15) or is a roof-only construction eligible for §11-4(3) no. 6. The answer changes the roof build-up.
2. Whether one qualifying escape window satisfies §11-13(4) for a one-room cabin.
3. Whether the technical cupboard needs EI 30 separation.
4. The §11-13 Table 1 travel distance for risk class 4, which the retrieved table does not list.
5. Whether the whole cabin counts as an escape route for §11-9 surface classes. D-s2,d0 keeps exposed reclaimed timber viable; B-s1,d0 would not.
6. Whether any wood-fibre insulation can be justified in the roof under §11-9 D no. 7 or by documented analysis.
