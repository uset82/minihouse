## 4. Interpretation

- Gross window area is **2.47 m²**; all sills are at or above 900 mm, so the qualifying area is also **2.47 m²** before frame reduction.
- With no frame reduction (ff=1.00), the minimum LT is **0.510**. At ff=0.85 it rises to **0.600**; at ff=0.80, **0.638**.
- If the actual glazing LT or frame factor is lower, the design needs more glass, better glazing, or the general daylight-factor route (average DF >= 2.0 % with CIE 171:2006-validated simulation).
- The door may add qualifying glass if its glazing is documented; it is conservatively excluded here.
- The simplified route depends on the final legal use being the mikrohus/dwelling route; if C-01 lands on a temporary building, confirm §13-7 applicability with the municipality.
