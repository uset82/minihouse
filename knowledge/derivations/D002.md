# D002 · Corner lifting-node load path and failure modes

Status: established from primary sources (Stage 3), 2026-11-16. Sources: S007, S006, S000, S008.

## Complete load path (crane hook to chassis)

1. Crane hook / spreader or cross-beam
2. Sling leg(s) — tension T = V/sin α; horizontal H = V·cot α (α from horizontal)
3. Lifting eye / ear — hole bearing, net section, plate bending; eccentricity e gives M_e = F·e
4. Weld or bolt group connecting eye plate to bracket/stiffener — direct force + moment distributed over actual group geometry
5. Bracket plate + triangular stiffeners — plate bending, buckling of stiffened panel
6. Bracket-to-profile connection (screws or weld) — bearing/pull-out/shear, screw group failure
7. Profile wall (RHS/SHS or LGS channel) — local wall deformation, distortion, bearing
8. Cross member / transverse beam at the node — force transferred into the frame
9. Longitudinal stringer — global bending and torsion of the chassis
10. Rest of the structure — aperture distortion, corner twist, load sharing with other legs

An element is only as strong as the weakest link. A test on an isolated plate (steps 3–4 only) does not qualify the node.

## Failure modes to check (≥ 8)

| # | Failure mode | Where | Standard / guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Net section tension across hole | eye plate | EN 1993-1-8 |
| 2 | Gross section yielding | eye plate | EN 1993-1-1 |
| 3 | Bearing / hole ovalisation | eye plate, pin | EN 1993-1-8 |
| 4 | Block tearing / shear-out | eye plate around hole group | EN 1993-1-8 |
| 5 | Plate bending from eccentricity | eye plate, bracket | EN 1993-1-8, M_e = F·e |
| 6 | Weld throat failure (direct + moment) | eye-to-bracket weld group | EN 1993-1-8 — not F/(aL) alone |
| 7 | Bolt/screw shear and tension | bracket-to-profile | EN 1993-1-8 |
| 8 | Profile local wall deformation / bearing | RHS/SHS or channel wall | EN 1993-1-1/-1-5 |
| 9 | Stiffener yielding / buckling | bracket stiffeners | EN 1993-1-5 |
| 10 | Interposed beam distortion from eccentric eye | beam between eye and bracket | S007 observed this below design load |
| 11 | Fixing screw pull-out, nearly simultaneous | bracket-to-stud group | S007 D2 failure mode |
| 12 | Bracket weld fracture | folded plate weld | S007 D1 failure mode |

## Experimental evidence from S007

- D1 (welded bracket, eccentric S1 eye): weld fracture + beam distortion from eccentricity.
- D2 (stiffened bracket, screw connection): sudden screw pull-out at peak.
- Asymmetric eye caused distortion **below** the 17.5 kN design load; symmetric eye was stiffer and selected for the real module.
- Peak-load CoV ≈ 10%, displacement CoV ≈ 45% — measure and report both.

## Design consequence for MiniHouse

- Detail the eye so the load line passes through the profile centroid where possible (`e → 0`), or quantify M_e explicitly.
- Test the full path, with at least one eccentric variant, in displacement control to failure.
- Fix specimen count and acceptance criteria before testing (rules.md §5).
- All outputs remain CONCEPTUAL until the responsible engineer releases them.
