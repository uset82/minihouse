# S006 · JRC — Design of joints in steel and composite structures

- **Title:** Design of joints in steel and composite structures
- **Authors:** Jean-Pierre Jaspart and Klaus Weynand
- **Publisher:** ECCS (European Convention for Constructional Steelwork), 2016, 1st edition
- **URL:** https://eurocodes.jrc.ec.europa.eu/publications/design-joints-steel-and-composite-structures
- **Type:** book & designer guide
- **Access:** free metadata / purchase link (verified 2026-11-16). Book itself **to acquire lawfully**.

## Key claims

- Explains the design rules for connections in Eurocode 3 **Part 1-8 "Design of joints"** (and EN 1994-1-1 for composite).
- Covers mechanical fasteners, welded connections, simple joints, moment-resisting joints, lattice girder joints.
- Joint configurations: beam-to-column, beam-to-beam, column bases, beam/column splices.
- Load cases: axial, shear, bending and combinations.
- Briefly covers fire, fatigue, seismic and exceptional loading / progressive collapse.
- Includes worked examples and references to design tools.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

This is the authoritative ECCS guide for the corner-node and lifting-ear design in Fase 3 — the exact place where the report says a real module can fail. Confirms EN 1993-1-8 is the joint design standard.

## Limits and caveats

It is a general Eurocode guide, not a Norwegian National Annex and not an ear-specific test method. The McAvov/QUB test programme (Stage 3) supplies the experimental side.
