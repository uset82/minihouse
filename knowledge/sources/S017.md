- **Title:** Lifting Operation Risk Management Guide
- **Publisher:** ESTA — European Association of Abnormal Transport and Mobile Cranes
- **Document:** GD25001-EN, 36 pp.
- **URL:** https://estaeurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ESTA-GUIDELINE-GD25001-EN-final.pdf
- **Full text:** `knowledge/refs/ESTA-GD25001-lifting-guideline.pdf` (downloaded 2026-11-16)
- **Access:** free PDF
- **Nature:** industry best-practice guidance; **not a regulation or standard** and not a lift authorisation.

## Key claims relevant to MiniHouse

- §3: a load may only be lifted if structurally sound during the lift, with no loose parts, no shifting COG, and no damage to lifting accessories or the load.
- §3.2: a lifting plan requires the load mass and centre of gravity, load dimensions, number/capacity/position of lifting points, and the wind resistance coefficient/shape of the load.
- §3.4.1: lifting points below the centre of gravity are a **critical lift**; the sling stability triangle projected down to the lifting points must contain the COG in every direction.
- §3.4: if exposed sailing area exceeds roughly **1.2 m² per tonne** of load, crane-chart wind limits are no longer valid and the operating company must calculate a reduced permissible wind speed.
- §4: defines competent roles: Person in Charge/lift planner, lift supervisor, lift engineer, operator, signaller, rigger/slinger.
- §5.3: a full engineered lift plan comprises a **lifting arrangement**, a **rigging arrangement** and a **(dis)assembly arrangement**, with status and revision control.
  - Lifting arrangement: crane properties, maximum allowed wind speed, top and side views, load weight/COG/dimensions, pick-up/set-down positions, radius, lifting path, ground bearing pressure and clearances.
  - Rigging arrangement: material list, load data, lifting-beam dimensions/sections, rigging stability, front and side views.
- §6.1: pre-use walkaround inspection for wear, cracks and damage; do not operate equipment that fails inspection.
- §6.2–6.3: site restrictions, ground conditions, communications, weather, risk assessment, method statement, toolbox talk, and management of change before restarting after changes.

## Limits and caveats

- European industry guidance; Norwegian legal duties still come from authoritative Norwegian law (S013) and the competent lifting company's approved plan.
- It does not size MiniHouse steel, welds, lugs or slings and does not replace independent structural calculation.
- Some numeric thresholds, such as the 1.2 m²/t wind ratio, are guidance values to be confirmed by the crane operating company for the actual crane and load.
