# S020 · TEK17 §1-2 — application to specific measures (use classification)

- **Title:** Byggteknisk forskrift (TEK17) § 1-2 Forskriftens anvendelse på særskilte tiltak
- **URL:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/1/1-2
- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official; guidance last changed 01.07.2023
- **Verified:** 2026-11-16
- **Related official page:** DIBK mikrohus wizard, https://dibk.no/verktoy-og-veivisere/mikrohus-som-helarsbolig

## Binding text relevant to MiniHouse

- §1-2(2): a detached residential building up to **30 m² BRA** with one dwelling unit, one storey, no basement and max **4.5 m above ground**, and a holiday home with one dwelling unit, shall satisfy the listed parts of TEK17: chapters 1–7, 9–11, 14, 15–17, plus only specified provisions of chapters 8, 12 and 13.
- §1-2(4): TEK17 applies to permanent and temporary structures/installations, except chapters 8, 12, 13 and 14 apply only “så langt de passer” (so far as applicable).
- §1-2(5): for **temporary buildings**, TEK17 applies except chapters 8, 12 and 13 apply so far as applicable; in chapter 14, **only §14-3** applies.
- Guidance to §1-2(2): energy requirements differ by category. Detached residential building ≤30 m² → §14-5 first paragraph; one-unit holiday home → §14-5 second or third paragraph. Ordinary residential buildings outside the provision are subject to TEK17 in full.

## Consequences for MiniHouse

“Emergency cabin” is not a TEK17 category. The applicable branch must be decided by the municipality and recorded in C-01:

1. detached year-round dwelling ≤30 m² one unit → §1-2(2) route;
2. one-unit holiday home → §1-2(2) route, but §14-5 energy branch differs;
3. temporary building / brakke for a limited period → §1-2(5) route;
4. other use → likely full TEK17 or another regime.

Do not apply the mikrohus simplification until branch 1 is confirmed. Do not use branch 3 to avoid structural safety, fire safety or competent sign-off.

## Limits

- TEK17 §1-2 classifies technical requirements; it does not by itself grant planning permission.
- Plan- og bygningsloven, municipal plans, access, water/sewage, waste, neighbour distance and visual quality still apply (S018).
