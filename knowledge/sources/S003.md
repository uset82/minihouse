# S003 · Steel Construction Info — Design codes and standards

- **Title:** Design codes and standards
- **URL:** https://steelconstruction.info/Design_codes_and_standards
- **Publisher:** Steel Construction Institute (SCI), UK
- **Access:** free, open web (verified 2026-11-16; note redirect from www.)

## Key claims

- Eurocodes cover EN 1990–EN 1999; published first generation 2002–2007.
- Each Eurocode Part has a main body, normative annexes and informative annexes; the relevance of informative annexes is decided nationally.
- Eurocode text is split into **Principles** (must be followed) and **Application Rules** (standard route; alternatives allowed if principles are met).
- National Annex is "an essential document when using a Eurocode Part" — it can set factor values, modify limits or formulas, choose design methods and decide informative annex use.
- First generation in force until **March 2028**; second generation expected **early 2028**, including reuse/retrofit of existing structures and stronger robustness requirements.
- NCCI (non-contradictory complementary information) may be referenced by an NA but cannot be inside it; NCCI documents are informative, not Standards.

## Limits and caveats — IMPORTANT

This is **UK** guidance. It explains Eurocode structure well, but its National Annex statements and NCCI sources apply to the UK, **not Norway**. For MiniHouse the Norwegian NA (NS-EN …) and DIBK/Standard Norge sources govern. Never take a γ or ψ value from this page.
