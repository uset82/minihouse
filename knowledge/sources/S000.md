## Key claims

1. The concept is viable but must not move from plausible drawing to fabrication without explicit verification.
2. Treat the module simultaneously as building, transported structure, secured load, temporarily suspended structure and — if a bespoke accessory is used — a lifting system.
3. Architecture of verification: reproducible hand calculation → independent FEM → representative physical test. Convergence alone is not proof.
4. Illustrative 3,000 kg / two 6 m stringers / q = 2.4525 kN/m / EI = 1.68 MN·m²: δ = 24.634 mm at end supports, 1.490 mm with supports inset 1.2 m.
5. Four supports or four sling legs never imply W/4 — three equilibrium equations, four unknowns; distribution depends on stiffness, rigging geometry, tolerances and pre-tension.
