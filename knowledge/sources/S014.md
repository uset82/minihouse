# S014 · Norwegian National Annex access — investigation result

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Question:** Can the Norwegian National Annex values (γ, ψ, α and other nationally determined parameters) for NS-EN 1990/1991/1993/1995 be obtained legally and freely?

## Finding

**No.** The Norwegian Eurocodes and their National Annexes are published by Standard Norge and distributed through `standard.no` / `online.standard.no` as **paid standards**. The DIBK guidance pages (S005, S011, S012) repeatedly link to `online.standard.no` for NS 3031:2014, NS-EN ISO 9972, NS-EN ISO 10211, NS-EN 12828 — all behind purchase. Attempts to reach a Standard Norge NS-EN 1990 page returned 404 (site restructure); general web search produced no authoritative free table of Norwegian NA coefficients.

In the UK, SCI publishes National Annex guidance freely (S003), but those values are explicitly country-specific and **cannot be transferred to Norway**.

## Consequence

Any Eurocode coefficient needed for MiniHouse (γ_G, γ_Q, ψ_0/1/2, γ_M0/M1/M2, buckling curve selections, snow/wind national parameters) remains **BLOCKED** until the specific NS-EN parts and their Norwegian National Annexes are purchased or accessed through an authorised licence. The clause map (clause-map.md) is correct at part level but contains no coefficients by design.

## What can proceed without the NA

- D001/D003 hand calculations (equilibrium and geometry — no code factors).
- Full load-path and failure-mode reasoning (D002).
- Geometry, mass/CG inventory and calculator.
- Qualitative transport/lift planning.
- All work explicitly labelled conceptual.

## What cannot proceed

- Member sizing, joint verification, stability checks to Eurocode.
- Any claim of code compliance.
- Fabrication release.
