# S005 · TEK17 §10-2 — Konstruksjonssikkerhet

- **Title:** Forskrift om tekniske krav til byggverk (TEK17), § 10-2
- **URL:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/10/10-2
- **Publisher:** Direktoratet for byggkvalitet (DIBK), Norway
- **Access:** free, official (verified 2026-11-16)

## Key requirement (direct quote, subsection 2)

> "Byggverket skal prosjekteres og utføres slik at det oppnås tilfredsstillende sikkerhet mot brudd og tilstrekkelig stivhet og stabilitet for laster som kan oppstå under forutsatt bruk. Kravet gjelder byggverk under utførelse og i endelig tilstand."

Translation: the building must be designed and executed to achieve satisfactory safety against failure and sufficient stiffness and stability for loads that may arise under assumed use. **The requirement applies during execution AND in the final state.**

## Other requirements

- Materials/products must have properties satisfying mechanical resistance and stability; strength properties must be **known and documented**, and must not deteriorate over time to endanger safety.
- Compliance route: NS-EN 1990 (basis) + NS-EN 1991…1999 with applicable **Norwegian national annexes**. Equivalent alternative methods are possible but the guidance calls their documentation "svært krevende" — in practice the Norwegian Standard route is the realistic one.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

The "under execution" clause is the direct legal hook for treating lifting, transport and assembly as explicit design situations rather than hiding them in the permanent building case. The "known and documented" material clause is the hook for reclaimed timber traceability.

## Limits and caveats

TEK17 is the building regulation; it does not replace the Eurocode calculations, the National Annex coefficients, Arbeidstilsynet lift rules, or Statens vegvesen transport rules.
