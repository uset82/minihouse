## §13-2 — Ventilation in dwellings

| Provision | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Whole dwelling when occupied | min **1.2 m³/h per m² floor area** |
| Bedrooms | min **26 m³/h fresh air per planned bed place** |
| Rooms not for permanent occupancy | min **0.7 m³/h per m² floor area** |
| Kitchen, toilet, wet room | extract ventilation with satisfactory efficiency |

Pre-accepted extract volumes (basic / boosted): kitchen 36 / 108 m³/h; bathroom 54 / 108; toilet 36 / 36; utility 36 / 72. Supply air must equal extract volume in boosted mode. Rates must be achievable with closed windows.

## §13-9 — General moisture requirement

Formal: "Grunnvann, overvann, nedbør, bruksvann og luftfuktighet skal ikke trenge inn og gi fuktskader, soppdannelse eller andre hygieniske problemer." Pre-accepted: moisture-sensitive products kept dry during storage, transport and construction; materials must tolerate expected moisture exposure. No NS-EN ISO 13788 reference on this page.

## §13-13 — Moisture from indoor air

Formal: assemblies must be designed so no moisture damage arises from condensed indoor water vapour.

Pre-accepted performance (exact requirements):
1. In insulated external walls/roofs and internal insulated hot/cold partitions there must be an **air- and vapour-tight layer, normally a separate vapour barrier, on the warm side of the insulation**.
2. The **wind barrier outside the insulation must be as vapour-open as possible and face a ventilated cavity** — the assembly then has self-drying capacity.
3. Vapour barrier vapour resistance **Sd ≥ 10 m**.
4. Vapour barrier and wind barrier joints must be **airtight**.
5. No layer of high vapour tightness elsewhere in the assembly than the vapour barrier (to ensure rapid drying).

## Relevance to MiniHouse

These are the legal counterpart to the moisture physics in S010. For the reclaimed-timber envelope: warm-side vapour barrier Sd ≥ 10 m, vapour-open wind barrier facing a ventilated cavity, airtight joints, and moisture-sensitive timber kept dry in storage/transport/construction. Ventilation: 18 m² × 1.2 = **21.6 m³/h** whole-module fresh air if occupied as a dwelling (C-01).
