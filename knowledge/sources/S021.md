# S021 · TEK17 §14-5 and §14-4 — energy exceptions and heating source

- **Title:** Byggteknisk forskrift (TEK17) §14-5 Unntak og krav til særskilte tiltak; §14-4 Krav til løsninger for energiforsyning
- **URLs:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/14/14-5 and /14/14-4
- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official; §14-5 guidance last changed 01.10.2020
- **Verified:** 2026-11-16

## Exact rules retrieved

- §14-5(1): `For frittstående bygning til og med 70 m2 oppvarmet BRA gjelder i dette kapitlet kun § 14-1, § 14-3 og § 14-4 første ledd.`
- §14-5(2): `For fritidsbolig til og med 70 m2 oppvarmet BRA gjelder ikke kravene i kapittel 14.`
- §14-5(3): for holiday homes over 70 m² to 150 m², only §14-1, §14-3 and §14-4 first paragraph apply.
- §14-4(1): `Det er ikke tillatt å installere varmeinstallasjon for fossilt brensel.` Renewable biofuels such as wood, pellets, bio-oil and biogas are not fossil fuels; process plants and emergency generators are outside this provision.
- Guidance to §14-5(1): the exception still applies to units assembled for a limited-period need, e.g. `anleggsbrakker`, cf. pbl. §30-5. Brakker/modules that become permanent buildings must meet the full energy requirements. Relocation of an existing brakke to the same use does not trigger upgrade to current energy requirements.

## Important correction to earlier MiniHouse notes

The earlier note that the §14-2 småhus frame `100 + 1600/A ≈ 188.9 kWh/m²·yr` governs an 18 m² MiniHouse was **too broad**. For a detached building up to **70 m² heated BRA**, §14-5(1) says only §14-1, §14-3 and §14-4(1) apply; **§14-2 does not apply**. For a one-unit holiday home up to 70 m², chapter 14 does not apply at all. For temporary buildings under §1-2(5), chapter 14 only §14-3 applies. The 188.9 figure remains arithmetic from §14-2 only, not an applicable MiniHouse requirement under the likely routes.

## What still applies likely

- §14-3 absolute minimum U-values and airtightness for the detached-building route: wall ≤0.22, roof ≤0.18, floor ≤0.18, windows/doors ≤1.2 W/m²K, air leakage ≤1.5 h⁻¹ at 50 Pa.
- §14-4(1) no fossil-fuel heating installation.
- Basic structural safety, fire safety, moisture and indoor-climate requirements from other chapters remain unaffected.

## Limits

- Final classification still depends on C-01 and the municipality (S020).
- NS 3031 remains relevant for documenting heat-loss/thermal-bridge values where required, but the full §14-2 energy-frame calculation is not triggered by the small detached-building route.
