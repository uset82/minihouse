# S018 · DIBK — Rules for mikrohus til boligformål

- **Title:** Regler for mikrohus til boligformål
- **Publisher:** Direktoratet for byggkvalitet (DIBK), Norway
- **URL:** https://www.dibk.no/byggtekniske-omrader/regler-for-mikrohus-til-boligformal
- **Access:** free, official; page last changed 03.07.2023 and rules in force from 01.07.2023
- **Verified:** 2026-11-16

## Key rules retrieved

- A **mikrohus** is a detached residential building up to **30 m² BRA**, with one dwelling unit.
- One storey only, no basement, and maximum **4.5 m above ground at any point**.
- Internal room height may be reduced to **2.2 m**.
- Exemptions include accessibility, stair, storage, sound/vibration and outlook requirements; ventilation must still secure satisfactory indoor air quality.
- Basic safety requirements still apply: structural safety, fire safety and ability to resist natural actions.
- Required main functions: indoor toilet, sleeping possibility, food preparation/storage (kitchen), bathroom function and living area.
- Minimum energy level must be met; documentation of compliance and of building-product properties is required.
- For a mikrohus on a built property, a responsible enterprise is not normally required under SAK §3-1(e); exception: chimney/fireplace. Municipal planning and building law still applies.
- Municipality may restrict placement; satisfactory waste solution, access, parking, water/sewage, safe building ground, neighbour distances and visual quality still apply.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

A 6 × 3 m module (18 m²) is within the mikrohus size envelope **if** it is legally a detached year-round dwelling with one dwelling unit. Carlos has declared use as an "emergency cabin", but its legal classification is still open (C-01). Therefore these rules are **conditional**, not a declared applicable regime.

## Limits and caveats

- This page does not classify MiniHouse. It describes a regime that may apply after the municipality/use classification is confirmed.
- The simplified responsibility route is not a waiver of structural safety, fire safety, natural actions, documentation, or municipal planning constraints.
- Exact Bergen municipal requirements remain project-specific and unknown.
