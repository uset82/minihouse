# S013 · Arbeidstilsynet — work equipment and lifting competence (Lovdata)

- **Title:** Forskrift om utførelse av arbeid … (FOR-2011-12-06-1357), chapters 10–13
- **URL:** https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2011-12-06-1357
- **Publisher:** Arbeids- og inkluderingsdepartementet / Lovdata (authoritative legal text)
- **Access:** free, official (verified 2026-11-16; regulation last changed FOR-2026-09-29-1949)

## Verified sections

- **§10-1** — Where the employer's risk assessment finds work equipment requires special caution, only workers with documented safety training per §10-2 may use it. Training may be given by the employer or another competent party.
- **§10-2** — Documented practical and theoretical training covering construction, operation, properties/use area, maintenance and control, plus the regulatory and instruction-manual requirements for safe use. Documentation must be issued, available to the safety representative and shown to authorities on demand. Workers may only be assigned within the area they were trained for. Repair demonstration exemption. EEA/Swiss citizens need Arbeidstilsynet permission for §10-3 equipment.
- **§10-3** — **Certified safety training** is required for: overhead/travelling cranes where use risks life/health; tower cranes; mobile cranes >2 tm; portal cranes; cranes >2 tm mounted on trucks/trailers; lift trucks with permanent driver position; earth-moving machines >15 kW; offshore cranes.
- **§11-4** — Traffic rules must be prepared and observed where mobile work equipment is used; organisational measures to keep pedestrians out of the work area.
- **§13-1** — Competent control (sakkyndig kontroll) by a competent business is required for, among others: **work equipment for lifting suspended loads**, lifting gear, lift trucks, work platforms, scaffolding, etc.
- **§13-3** — Competent control before first use and after each new assembly/setup where safety depends on assembly — explicitly includes foundation/setup of tower/portal/overhead cranes and cranes >2 tm mounted on vehicles.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

The crane for the module will almost certainly be a mobile crane >2 tm → **certified safety training (§10-3)**. Lifting gear and the module's own ears/spreader fall under **competent control (§13-1)** if used for suspended loads. If the spreader is assembled on site, §13-3 applies. This strengthens the lift-plan checklist's authority section.
