# S026 · TEK17 Chapter 7 — safety against natural hazards

- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official; retrieved 2026-11-17
- **Base index:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/7
- **Sections:** §7-1, §7-2, §7-3, §7-4 (chapter has no roman-numeral segment)
- **Scope driver:** TEK17 §1-2(2)(a) — chapters 1 to 7 apply **in full** on the mikrohus route (S020)

## Why this chapter matters more than its length suggests

Chapter 7 has four sections and almost no numbers that go into a detail drawing. It
is nevertheless the chapter that can **eliminate a site** before any of the cabin
design matters. For a Bergen plot that is not hypothetical: steep terrain, heavy
rainfall, marine-limit clay and a long coastline are all present in the municipality.

**§7-1 also settles a boundary that is easy to get wrong.** Its guidance states that
**snow, wind and seismic loads are not chapter 7**. Those are design loads handled
under **§10-2** and the Eurocode standards. Chapter 7 is for the loads you avoid by
*placing* the building — flood and landslide — not the ones you design for. So:

| Load | Governing route |
|---|---|
| Snow | §10-2 + NS-EN 1991-1-3 with Norwegian NA |
| Wind | §10-2 + NS-EN 1991-1-4 with Norwegian NA |
| Seismic | §10-2 + NS-EN 1998 with Norwegian NA |
| **Flood, storm surge** | **§7-2, safety classes F1–F3** |
| **Landslide, rockfall, debris flow, quick clay** | **§7-3, safety classes S1–S3** |
| Mountain landslide and its flood wave | §7-4, conditional exception |

That distinction matters because it prevents double-counting: a Bergen site can fail
§7-3 on landslide while the wind and snow design proceeds normally under §10-2.

## §7-1 — general requirement

> «(1) Byggverk skal plasseres, prosjekteres og utføres slik at det oppnås
> tilfredsstillende sikkerhet mot skade eller vesentlig ulempe fra naturpåkjenninger.»

> «(2) Tiltak skal prosjekteres og utføres slik at byggverk, byggegrunn og tilstøtende
> terreng ikke utsettes for fare for skade eller vesentlig ulempe som følge av tiltaket.»

Two distinct duties. Paragraph (1) is about the building being exposed to nature;
paragraph (2) is about **the building creating or worsening a hazard** for itself,
the ground and neighbouring terrain. Paragraph (2) is the one a cabin on a slope or
on fill can breach even when the site looks acceptable — and the guidance notes it
also covers protective works built off the plot, and that it does **not** cover
ground danger caused by nature alone.

The guidance also points to local climate conditions and to the county climate
profiles (Norsk klimaservicesenter).

## §7-2 — flood and storm surge

> «(2) For byggverk i flomutsatt område skal det fastsettes sikkerhetsklasse for flom
> etter tabellen under. Byggverk skal plasseres, dimensjoneres eller sikres mot flom
> slik at største nominelle årlige sannsynlighet i tabellen ikke overskrides.»

| Safety class | Consequence | Largest nominal annual probability |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | small | **1/20** |
| **F2** | medium | **1/200** |
| **F3** | large | **1/1000** |

Pre-accepted class assignment:

- **F1** — buildings with little person occupancy and small economic or societal
  consequences: garage, warehouse with little occupancy.
- **F2** — most buildings intended for person occupancy, explicitly including
  **«bolig, fritidsbolig og campinghytte»**, plus garages/barracks, schools and
  kindergartens, offices, industrial buildings.
- **F3** — vulnerable societal functions: nursing homes, hospitals, fire stations,
  police stations, civil-defence facilities, infrastructure of major societal
  importance, waste sites where flooding could cause pollution.

**MiniHouse is F2 → 1/200.** That holds for both the dwelling and the holiday-home
reading, because the F2 list names both.

Three further points from the guidance that matter for a small building:

- Where a flood area would have **great depth or strong current**, the guidance says
  the **F3 level should be used** instead of F2. A cabin is light and buoyant
  relative to its volume, so this is a real consideration, not a technicality.
- The requirement can be met by **placing outside** the area, by **securing** against
  flooding, or by **designing the building to withstand** inundation without danger
  to people or major material damage. Raising the ground, building without a
  basement, or flood walls are named as securing measures.
- §7-2(3) allows **organisational flood protection** in special cases, but only with
  all conditions in letters a–f met, including a documented permanent 1/50 safety.
  It is a municipal instrument, not a designer's shortcut.

**The storm-surge clause and sea-level rise.** §7-2 covers **flom og stormflo**, and
the guidance explicitly requires that expected **climate change and sea-level rise**
be taken into account for storm surge. A cabin near the Bergen shoreline therefore
cannot be assessed against today's sea level alone; the flood level has to reflect
the building's service life. No number is quoted here because the datum depends on
the site (C-01) and on Kartverket's sea-level data.

## §7-3 — landslide

> «(2) For byggverk i skredfareområde skal det fastsettes sikkerhetsklasse for skred
> etter tabellen under. Byggverk og tilhørende uteareal skal plasseres, dimensjoneres
> eller sikres mot skred, herunder sekundærvirkninger av skred, slik at største
> nominelle årlige sannsynlighet … ikke overskrides.»

| Safety class | Consequence | Largest nominal annual probability |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | small | **1/100** |
| **S2** | medium | **1/1000** |
| **S3** | large | **1/5000** |

Pre-accepted class assignment:

- **S1** — buildings where people normally are not present and consequences are
  small: garage, outhouse, boat house, small piers, warehouse with little occupancy.
- **S2** — normally **at most 25 persons**, or medium economic/societal consequences,
  explicitly including **«enebolig, tomannsbolig og eneboliger i
  kjede/rekkehus/boligblokk/fritidsbolig med maksimum 10 boenheter»**.
- **S3** — more than 25 persons, or large consequences: housing with **more than 10
  dwelling units**, schools, kindergartens, nursing homes, local emergency
  institutions.

**MiniHouse is S2 → 1/1000.** One dwelling unit, one or two occupants: far inside
the S2 description.

Two refinements that help a small project:

- For **S2** buildings the requirement for the **associated outdoor area may be
  reduced to the S1 level (1/100)**. The reason given is that exposure time outdoors
  is shorter, so the risk to life and health is lower.
- Buildings may be **designed to withstand landslide forces** where the loads are not
  too large — the guidance puts the practical ceiling at approximately **50 kPa**.
  Above that, siting or protective works are the only routes.

**Quick clay is a separate, stricter regime.** §7-3 adds that for areas with
**kvikkleireskred** an equivalent safety level must be set, and the guidance expands
this into *områdeskred* — area landslides in quick clay and other soils with brittle
failure behaviour, per NVE guide 1/2019. Three points that apply directly here:

- Area landslides can be **far larger than the individual project**; a hazard zone can
  extend well beyond the plot.
- **Below the marine limit the ground must be investigated for landslide hazard even
  outside identified quick-clay hazard zones.** Bergen has marine-limit terrain, so
  this is a live requirement, not a formality.
- The required investigation depends on a **tiltakskategori** (K0, K1, K2, K3…) set
  from the consequence for the project. The guidance's K0 examples are small
  interventions with very limited ground works and no influx of people: garages, boat
  houses, extensions to existing buildings, detached outhouses, tool sheds. **A new
  dwelling is not in that list**, so a cabin placed as a dwelling cannot be assumed
  to be K0. The category has to be set per case.

## §7-4 — mountain landslide and flood wave

A conditional exception permitting development in areas exposed to **fjellskred** and
the **flood wave** it generates, even where §7-3(2) is not met, provided:

- **(a)** the development is settled in an area plan, with documented absence of
  alternative safe building land and decisive societal importance; and
- **(b)** personal safety is secured by a proper emergency system based on periodic or
  real-time monitoring, warning and evacuation, with **warning time not shorter than
  72 hours** and **evacuation time at most 12 hours**.

This is an instrument for NVE-monitored mountain areas and regional planning. It is
**not** a route for a private cabin, and it should not be invoked as one.

## What this means for MiniHouse

The chapter does not constrain the cabin; it constrains the **plot**. Three
consequences:

1. **The hazard classes are already known and do not depend on C-01.** F2 (1/200) and
   S2 (1/1000) apply to a dwelling *and* to a holiday home, exactly as Chapter 11 did
   for fire (S024). What remains is the site.
2. **Two site facts decide most of it:** whether the plot is in a mapped flood or
   landslide hazard area, and whether it lies **below the marine limit** (quick clay).
   Both are answerable from public maps before any design work.
3. **A transportable building does not get a discount.** TEK17 assesses the building
   in its placed position. The fact that the cabin *can* be moved does not reduce the
   requirement at the site where it stands — though it does mean the assessment must
   be repeated for every new site over the cabin's life.

The screening procedure is in [cribado de riesgos naturales](../../fase1/cribado-riesgos-naturales.md).

## Limits

- No hazard map has been consulted. This card records the **requirements**, not a
  finding about any plot.
- The safety classes are nominal annual probabilities of *damaging* events as defined
  in the guidance; they are not predictions for a specific site.
- §7-2's sea-level-rise clause and §7-3's quick-clay regime both require specialist
  investigation (NVE methodology, geotechnical report) that has not been done and
  cannot be done by the agent.
- Nothing here is a geotechnical assessment, a flood assessment or a siting approval.
