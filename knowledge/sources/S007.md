- **Venue:** CERI 2018, University College Dublin, 29–30 Aug 2018; 6 pages (7 with cover)

## Key technical data

- Light-gauge steel modular house prototype (McAvoy Group, modules manufactured Dec 2017; final house = three bedrooms, modules successfully lifted and connected).
- **Design vertical load per lifting point: 17.5 kN.**
- **Six lifting points, three per side** — studs run full height and connect roof and ground beams.
- Stud sections: channel 150 × 65 × 1.2 mm (external), 64 × 42 × 1.2 mm (internal).
- Two bracket designs: **D1** (folded 4 mm plate inside stud, 12 self-tapping screws, top plate ø40 mm hole + welded nut for M16 eye); **D2** (folded plate + triangular stiffeners, 7 screws on 64 mm studs / 12 on 150 mm at 35 mm pitch).
- Two eye types: **S1** asymmetric handle (eccentricity ±25 mm, variable) and **S2** symmetric handle above bolt (eccentricity 0).
- Tests: Zwick Roell 100 kN universal machine, displacement control, 5 mm/min, specimens to **failure**.

## Test results (Table 2)

| Specimen group | Avg peak load | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 64 mm stud specimens (n=6) | 37.8 kN (CoV 10%) | peak displacement 7.4–23.4 mm, avg 14.2 mm (CoV 45%) |
| 150 mm stud specimens (n=6) | 54.2 kN (CoV 13%) | 5 of 6 in 51.4–61.2 kN; one reduced-screw specimen 42.8 kN |

All specimens reached **at least twice the 17.5 kN design load**.

## Failure modes observed

- **D1:** breaking of welds of the bracket plate; interposed beam distorted and bent due to eccentricity of the lifting-eye handle.
- **D2:** sudden failure from insufficient bracket-to-stud connection — fixing screws pulled off nearly simultaneously at peak load.
- **S1 (asymmetric eye):** distorted light-steel elements at loads **below** the design load; less ductile.
- **S2 (symmetric eye):** highest stiffness; D2+S2 selected for the actual project. Less bending/distortion.

## Eurocode citation — critical

The paper's abstract states verbatim: *"The lifting bracket was then designed with guidance from the European Standard EN 1993-1-2:2005."* JRC's official part list (S004) confirms 1-2 is **fire design**, not joints. The report's critique is therefore correct at the primary source. See C001.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

- Method transferable: identify full load path → build the real detail → test to failure → measure F–δ, permanent deformation and failure mode.
- Confirms eye eccentricity materially changes deformation and can cause damage below design load — directly relevant to MiniHouse's `M_e = F·e` requirement.
- Confirms testing must cover multiple bracket variants and both symmetric/asymmetric load application.
- Design load 17.5 kN belongs to **their** module — never import it to MiniHouse.

## Limits

LGS system, ~9 m modules, six lift points; different geometry and material from a 6 × 3 m steel-chassis module. Transfer the **method**, not the capacities.
