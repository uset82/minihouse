# S011 · TEK17 Chapter 14 — Energy (numeric requirements)

- **Title:** Byggteknisk forskrift (TEK17), Kapittel 14 Energi
- **URLs:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/14/14-2 and /14/14-3
- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official (verified 2026-11-16)

> **SCOPE CORRECTION 2026-11-16:** §14-5(1) exempts detached buildings up to 70 m² heated BRA from §14-2; only §§14-1, 14-3 and 14-4(1) apply. For a one-unit holiday home up to 70 m², chapter 14 does not apply. See S021. The §14-2 values below remain correctly transcribed but are **not the governing route** for the likely MiniHouse classifications.

## §14-2 — Energy efficiency requirement (values; applicability limited by §14-5)

Total net energy need must not exceed the framework **and** §14-3 minimums must be met. Calculation per **NS 3031:2014**. Small house ("småhus", definition per NS 3457-3:2013 — includes detached, 2–4 dwelling, terraced up to 3 storeys):

- Energy frame: **100 + 1600/A [kWh/m² heated BRA per year]**, where A = heated BRA in m².

Alternative "energy measures" route for dwellings (småhus / boligblokk):

| Measure | Småhus |
|---|---|
| U exterior wall | ≤ 0.18 W/m²K |
| U roof | ≤ 0.13 |
| U floor | ≤ 0.10 |
| U windows and doors | ≤ 0.80 |
| Window+door area / heated BRA | ≤ 25% |
| Annual avg temperature efficiency of heat recovery | ≥ 80% |
| Specific fan power SFP | ≤ 1.5 kW/(m³/s) |
| Air leakage number at 50 Pa | ≤ 0.6 h⁻¹ |
| Normalised thermal bridge value | ≤ 0.05 W/m²K |

Redistribution between measures allowed if heat loss number does not increase (per NS 3031:2014); SFP excluded.

## §14-3 — Minimum levels (ALWAYS fulfilled)

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| U exterior wall | ≤ 0.22 W/m²K |
| U roof | ≤ 0.18 |
| U floor on ground / against outside air | ≤ 0.18 |
| U window and door incl. frame | ≤ 1.2 |
| Air leakage at 50 Pa | ≤ 1.5 h⁻¹ |

Heating pipes/equipment/ducts must be insulated to economically optimal thickness (e.g. NS-EN 12828:2012+A1:2014 §4.8 / App C).

## Relevance to MiniHouse

**Corrected 2026-11-16:** a 6 × 3 m module is 18 m². The arithmetic `100 + 1600/18 ≈ 188.9 kWh/m²·yr` is a valid §14-2 calculation, but §14-5(1) means a detached building up to 70 m² heated BRA is exempt from §14-2; a one-unit holiday home up to 70 m² is exempt from the whole of chapter 14 (S020, S021). Therefore **188.9 is not a MiniHouse compliance target** under the likely routes. The §14-3 U-value and airtightness floors still apply for the detached-building route, and §14-4(1) prohibits fossil-fuel heating. Final classification remains C-01.

## Limits

Energy calculation must follow NS 3031:2014 (not freely available here). The numbers are legal requirements, not verified performance of this module — no energy model has been run.
