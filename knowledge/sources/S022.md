## Scope caveat (important)

The PDF states that the directive applies to vehicles/combinations in **international traffic** and that it can be freely used in Norway on 19.50 m roads in Bk10 when the directive is met. It also states that individual national provisions may be used within a country's borders and that Norwegian provisions may be used for both Norwegian and foreign vehicles in Norway. Therefore the 4.00 m general height is a strong screening value, **not a route-specific domestic permit or dispensation**. Carrier and Statens vegvesen must confirm the rule applicable to the actual transport.

## Relevance to MiniHouse

The R1 eave datum of 3000 mm plus any 900–1300 mm trailer/support stack produces loaded heights from about 4.03 m to 4.85 m. Even the lowest screening case is at/over 4.00 m. Options: reduce eave/ridge height, use a lower deck/support stack, or treat the move as special transport with route check/dispensation. This is a transport-envelope warning, not a final prohibition.
