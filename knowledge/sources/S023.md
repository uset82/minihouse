# S023 · TEK17 Chapter 13 — sections applicable to the mikrohus route

- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official; retrieved 2026-11-16
- **Base index:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/13
- **Scope driver:** TEK17 §1-2(2) and guidance (S020)

## Applicability correction

For the detached residential building ≤30 m² / mikrohus route, TEK17 §1-2(2)(e) lists only:

- §13-1 **first paragraph**
- §13-4, §13-5, §13-7
- §13-9 to §13-16

Therefore **§13-2, §13-3, §13-6 and §13-8 do not apply** on that route. §13-1 guidance explicitly states that a qualifying small detached dwelling is exempt from §13-2; alternative air quantities may be used but must be documented to give satisfactory indoor air quality.

## Verified requirements and numbers

### §13-1(1) — functional ventilation (applies)
`Bygningen skal ha ventilasjon som sikrer tilfredsstillende luftkvalitet` by being adapted to room design, intended use, pollution and moisture load; satisfactory odour; no pollutant concentrations causing health damage or irritation. This is outcome-based, not a fixed m³/h schedule.

### §13-4 — thermal indoor climate (applies)
- At least **one window or one door** must be openable to outside air in rooms for permanent occupancy.
- Guidance: keep air temperature below **22 °C** as far as possible when heating is needed; recommended operative temperatures **19–26 / 16–26 / 10–26 °C** by activity; foot-to-head difference above **3–4 °C** unacceptable; daily variation about **4 °C** unacceptable; a dwelling usually complies if **at least two** passive measures are used (external shading, openable cross-ventilation, thermal mass, reduced glazing on sun-exposed façades, etc.).

### §13-5 — radon (applies)
- Annual average radon concentration ≤ **200 Bq/m³**.
- Building with permanent-occupancy rooms must have a **radon barrier against the ground** and be prepared for pressure-reducing measures that can be activated when indoor radon exceeds **100 Bq/m³**, unless it is documented that these measures are unnecessary.
- Imported fill placed over the barrier must have documented low radon emission.

### §13-7 — light (applies)
- Satisfactory access to light; permanent-occupancy rooms need satisfactory daylight.
- General route: average daylight factor ≥ **2.0 %** with CIE 171:2006-validated simulation.
- Dwelling simplified route: `Ag ≥ 0.07 · ABRA / LT`, where Ag is glass area at least **0.8 m** above floor and not in a light well.
- Fritidsbolig simplified route: `Ag ≥ 0.1 · ABRA`.
- Horizon obstruction assumption: no screening above **45°** from horizontal.

### §13-9 to §13-13 — moisture (apply)
- §13-9: no groundwater/rainwater/use-water/humidity penetration causing moisture damage, mould or hygiene problems; moisture-sensitive products kept dry in storage, transport and construction.
- §13-10: capillary-breaking, pressure-breaking and draining layer under/against ground; if crushed rock is used, thickness ≥ **2 × material capillary rise height**; drained masses protected against silt (e.g. geotextile); below-ground surfaces water-repellent; below groundwater watertight.
- §13-11: terrain fall away from building; preaccepted **≥1:50 over at least 3 m** from wall face; roof water included; alternatives allowed on difficult sites.
- §13-12: façade cladding/windows/doors/penetrations must drain intruded rain and allow drying; two-stage seal with drained/ventilated cavity is preaccepted. Roof must have adequate fall and drainage; asphalt membranes and roof foils **≥1:40**; roof must avoid ponding, snow-melt damage and dangerous icing; runoff led away.
- §13-13: warm-side air/vapour barrier; vapour barrier `Sd ≥ 10 m`; vapour-open wind barrier toward ventilated cavity; airtight joints.

### §13-14 — built moisture (applies)
- Products/structures dry enough at installation or sealing to avoid mould, decay of organic materials or increased emissions.
- Moisture content **must be measured** to document compliance.
- Timber generally **<20 wt% moisture**; in low-drying structures **<15 wt% before installation**.

### §13-15 — wet rooms (applies if bathroom/shower exists)
- Wet room needs a drain, adequate fall to drain, visible leak water led to drain, waterproof layers protecting vulnerable backing constructions, and sealed penetrations.
- Shower fall options: **1:50 within at least 0.8 m** from drain; or recessed shower ≥**10 mm** with **1:100** fall; or whole bathroom floor **1:100**.
- Leak-water upstand at least **25 mm** at outer edges and **15 mm** at door opening when used instead of whole-floor fall; waterproof layer reaches at least **25 mm above top of drain grate** at outer edges.

### §13-16 — cleaning before use (applies)
Surfaces in rooms, ducts and similar must be cleaned before occupation and be free of visible dust and grease; NS-INSTA 800-1:2018 is referenced for assessment.

## Explicit non-applicability on likely mikrohus route

- §13-2 numeric ventilation rates are **not** the mikrohus requirement; see §13-1(1) guidance above.
- §13-3 public/work-building ventilation does not apply.
- §13-6 sound/vibration is exempt under §1-2(2).
- §13-8 outlook is exempt under §1-2(2).

## Limits

- If the final use is classified as a temporary building under §1-2(5), chapters 8, 12 and 13 apply only “så langt de passer”; confirm with the municipality (S020).
- If the use is an ordinary dwelling outside §1-2(2), full TEK17 applies and §13-2 rates return.
- Numeric values above are requirements or preaccepted solutions as stated on the official pages; they are not performance results for MiniHouse.
