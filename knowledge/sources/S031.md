# S031 · NS-EN ISO 9972 — the airtightness measurement, not held

- **Standard:** NS-EN ISO 9972:2015, *Thermal performance of buildings — Determination of air permeability of buildings — Fan pressurization method* (adopted in Norway as NS-EN ISO 9972:2015, replacing NS-EN 13829:2000).
- **Publisher:** Standard Norge / ISO.
- **Access:** **not acquired.** No copy is held, no page was retrieved, and nothing in this project has been read from it.
- **Verified status:** the standard's identity, title and subject are established — it is the fan-pressurisation method, and it supersedes NS-EN 13829. Its **content is not verified**.

## Why the card exists before the document

TEK17 §14-3 states a number — 1.5 h⁻¹ at 50 Pa — and a number of that kind is only reproducible if the method that produced it is named. The method lives in this standard. So the project has a requirement it cannot test correctly without a document it does not own, and the honest response is to record the gap rather than to write a procedure from memory.

This is the same class of gap as S019 (NS-EN 1090-2) and S014 (the National Annex): a paywalled standard that governs a claim the project wants to make. It is listed as *to acquire*, never reconstructed.

## What may be used

- The **identity and subject** of the standard, and that it replaced NS-EN 13829.
- The **existence of more than one test method** within it, and the fact that a test of the envelope with intentional openings sealed is not the same measurement as a test of the building as used. This is why `fase1/especificacion-ensayo-tetthet.md` requires the method to be named on the certificate rather than assumed.
- The requirement, from §2-3(1)'s guidance (S030), that the evidence be **verifiable in the project** — which is what makes a method-less number inadmissible.

## What may not be claimed

- **No clause number, point count, pressure range, temperature or wind limit, or uncertainty rule** is quoted from this standard anywhere in the project. Those are properties of a document nobody here has read.
- No test result is claimed, implied or extrapolated. **No measurement of this building exists.**
- No certificate, no conformity statement and no verification of §14-3 is made.

## Consequence

Row 14.5 of the ytelsesoversikt stays `undetermined` for two independent reasons: **the building does not exist to be tested**, and **the standard that defines the test is not held**. The specification in `fase1/especificacion-ensayo-tetthet.md` is written so that acquiring the standard changes the procedure's detail and not its conclusion — but the procedure's detail is exactly what a tester needs, so acquisition should precede the T1 test, not follow it.
