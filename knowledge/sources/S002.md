## Relevance to MiniHouse

Chapters 2, 4, 5 and 11 are directly useful: determinacy (the four-support / three-equation problem in the report), beam deflection (D001), and the matrix formulation behind Robot and PyNite.

## Limits and caveats

Introductory level — it does not cover Eurocode verification, torsion of open sections, joint design or dynamics. Treat as foundation only, not as a design authority.

## Used in

Stage 1 foundations; D001 background.
