## What image 1 shows ("From Base to Roof")

Exploded axonometric mini-site mockup with the heading "De la base al techo": a black steel chassis drawn as a flat ladder frame (two long rails, several cross members) separated below a reclaimed-timber volume; above it, a **two-slope (gable) metal roof in dark standing-seam finish**, floating. The timber volume has a door and a large window on the long façade and a smaller window on the end wall. Caption "Vista conceptual"; below, "Diseñada para moverse".

## What image 2 shows ("Norwegian slope")

Another mini-site mockup: the same cabin placed on a rocky slope beside a Norwegian fjord with mountains. Vertical reclaimed timber boards, dark metal **gable roof**, large window on the long façade, small high window on the gable end, dark base frame. Captions "Imagen conceptual" and "Cómo está hecha" with close-ups of the timber boards and the steel base corner.

## Consequences recorded

1. **Roof form:** gable, not the monopitch of R0 S-03. Pitch **not** taken from the images — must be dimensioned later.
2. **Chassis:** ladder frame with two rails + cross members is consistent with R0 axes.
3. **Envelope:** vertical reclaimed timber boards, dark metal roof, dark steel base — supports the rainscreen/ventilated-cavity strategy (S010, S012).
4. **Context:** Norwegian coastal/fjord setting consistent with Bergen market; reinforces wind-driven-rain detailing.
