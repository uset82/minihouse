# S024 · TEK17 Chapter 11 — fire safety for a single-storey dwelling

- **Publisher:** DIBK, Norway
- **Access:** free, official; retrieved 2026-11-17
- **Base index:** https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/11
- **Sections retrieved:** §§11-2, 11-3, 11-4, 11-6, 11-7, 11-8, 11-9, 11-10, 11-11, 11-12, 11-13, 11-14, 11-16, 11-17
- **Sections not retrieved:** §§11-1, 11-5 (explosion), 11-15 (livestock)

## Why this chapter matters for the C-01 ambiguity

Unlike Chapter 13 (ventilation) and Chapter 14 (energy), where the dwelling and
holiday-home routes diverge sharply, **Chapter 11 gives the same answer for both**:

| Intended use | Risikoklasse | Brannklasse, one storey |
|---|---|---|
| Bolig | 4 | BKL 1 |
| Fritidsbolig (incl. selvbetjente hytter, campinghytter, campingenheter) | 4 | BKL 1 |
| Arbeidsbrakke | 1 | no fire class assigned |
| Skur | 1 | no fire class assigned |

So the fire strategy is **not blocked by C-01** as long as the answer is "dwelling"
or "holiday home". It only changes if the cabin is finally classified as a
*arbeidsbrakke* or *skur*, which would drop it out of the fire classes entirely
(§11-3 Table 1 shows "–" for risk class 1 in one storey). That is an unlikely
classification for a habitable cabin with sleeping and cooking, but it is the one
branch that would change the answer, and it belongs in the C-01 question to the
municipality.

## §11-2 — Risikoklasser

Table 1 places **Bolig** and **Fritidsbolig** in **risikoklasse 4**.
**Arbeidsbrakke** and **Skur** are in **risikoklasse 1**.

Risk class 4 is defined by the criteria columns as: not intended for sporadic
occupancy, occupants know the escape conditions and can get themselves to safety,
**intended for overnight stays**, and assumed use entails **low fire hazard**.

Exception (pre-accepted performance no. 3): an activity that traditionally falls
in one class may *exceptionally, after separate assessment*, be placed in a lower
class if there are **few people** in the building and it is arranged for **rapid
and simple escape and rescue**. The assessment must be reasoned and documented.
This is the only route by which the cabin could be argued down from risk class 4,
and it would require a documented fire-engineering justification — not a
self-declaration.

## §11-3 — Brannklasser

Table 1: **risikoklasse 4 in one storey → BKL 1.**

Pre-accepted performance no. 3: a risk-class-4 residential building with **three
storeys** may still be BKL 1 **when each dwelling unit has a direct exit to
ground level**, without escaping via a stair or stairwell. Not needed for a
single-storey cabin, but it shows the regulator's logic: direct-to-ground escape
is what buys the lower class.

Risk class 1 in one storey is **not assigned a fire class at all** ("–"), and
§11-3 guidance states that no pre-accepted performance levels are given for that
case either.

## §11-4 — Bæreevne og stabilitet (load-bearing capacity and stability)

### Table 1, BKL 1 column

| Bygningsdel | BKL 1 |
|---|---|
| Bærende hovedsystem (main load-bearing system) | **R 30** [B 30] |
| Sekundære, bærende bygningsdeler, etasjeskillere og takkonstruksjoner som ikke er del av hovedbæresystem eller stabiliserende | **R 30** [B 30] |
| Trappeløp | – |
| Bærende bygningsdeler under øverste kjeller | R 60 A2-s1,d0 |
| Utvendig trappeløp | – |

### The governing exception — §11-4(3) no. 4

> «Byggverk i brannklasse 1 og risikoklasse 4 kan ha hoved- og sekundærbæresystem
> med brannmotstand **R 15**.»

This is the number that governs MiniHouse: **R 15**, not the R 30 in Table 1.
It applies to both the main and the secondary load-bearing system. R 15 is a
15-minute load-bearing resistance — enough for escape and rescue, not a
full-burn-out criterion. Compare §11-4(3) no. 3, which gives the same R 15 to
one-storey buildings in risk classes 2, 3 and 5.

### §11-4(3) no. 6 — roof construction without specified fire resistance

> «I byggverk uten loft eller med loft som bare kan benyttes som lager, kan
> takkonstruksjon oppføres uten spesifisert brannmotstand, forutsatt at denne
> ikke har avgjørende betydning for byggverkets stabilitet i rømningsfasen, og
> ett av følgende kriterier er tilstede:»

- **6.1** the roof construction is separated from the underlying floor by a
  fire-cell-dividing element dimensioned for **two-sided fire exposure**;
- **6.2** the building is BKL 1 and **all materials in the roof construction,
  including insulation, satisfy A2-s1,d0** (non-combustible);
- **6.3** the building is BKL 1 and the roof construction is protected from below
  with cladding **K210 B-s1,d0 [K1]**; **risk class 4 may use K210 D-s2,d0 [K2]**;
  the insulation must satisfy **A2-s1,d0**.

**This is a live design constraint for MiniHouse, not a formality.** The current
architectural intent is a reclaimed-timber envelope with a warm timber roof. Under
6.2 that intent is impossible: every roof material including insulation would have
to be non-combustible, which excludes timber rafters and wood-fibre insulation.
Under 6.3 the cladding may be K210 D-s2,d0 in risk class 4 — a limited-combustibility
class — but **the insulation still has to be A2-s1,d0**, so mineral wool rather
than wood fibre. Under 6.1 a fire-rated ceiling would have to separate a storage
loft from the room below.

### Open interpretation question (recorded, not resolved)

No. 6 speaks of the *roof construction*. In a gable where the rafters and ridge
also brace the walls and participate in global stability, the roof is arguably
part of the **main load-bearing system**, and no. 4 (R 15) would then govern
instead of no. 6. In a scheme where the roof carries only the roof, §11-4(5)
limits it to escape-phase capacity and no. 6 can apply. **Which reading applies
depends on the structural system that has not yet been chosen.** It must be
settled together with the fire engineer before the roof build-up is frozen, and
it must not be assumed in favour of the cheaper answer.

## §11-6 — Fire spread between buildings

- **Low building** = eave or ridge height **≤ 9.0 m** measured on the wall facing
  the neighbour. MiniHouse at ~3.2 m is a low building.
- **Minimum mutual distance 8.0 m** between low buildings, unless measures prevent
  spread within the escape and rescue time of the other building.
- **The provision does not apply to low buildings that together constitute one
  bruksenhet** — relevant if the cabin is an annex to an existing dwelling on the
  same property.
- Distance may be **less than 8.0 m** when the buildings are separated by
  fire-cell-dividing elements that together give equivalent resistance.
- Risk class 1, gross area **≤ 50 m²**, low or medium fire energy: may stand
  closer without special measures; **below 2.0 m** between different bruksenheter
  a fire-cell-dividing element is required.
- **Camping units** on legally established campsites: distance may be reduced to
  **4.0 m**, provided total area per unit **≤ 75 m²** and no point exceeds **4.0 m**
  above ground. This is a real option for the "emergency cabin" reading if the
  site is a campsite — but it is a siting rule, not a building classification.
- **Brannvegg** resistance by specific fire energy: **REI 120-M** up to 400 MJ/m²,
  **REI 180-M** 400–600, **REI 240-M** 600–800. It must extend **≥ 0.5 m above the
  highest adjoining roof** unless that roof is at least **EI 60 A2-s1,d0**.
- §11-6(3): when low buildings stand **closer than 8.0 m**, gross area per storey
  must not exceed the §11-7 Table 1 limits unless separated by a brannvegg.

**Consequence for siting in Bergen:** the 8.0 m rule, not the cabin itself, may
decide whether a tight plot works. It belongs in the C-01/C-02 site questions.

## §11-7 — Brannseksjoner (fire sections)

Table 1, maximum gross area per storey without sectioning:

| Specific fire energy | Normal | With alarm | With sprinkler | With smoke venting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Over 400 MJ/m² | 800 m² | 1 200 m² | 5 000 m² | not suitable |
| 50–400 MJ/m² | 1 200 m² | 1 800 m² | 10 000 m² | 4 000 m² |
| Under 50 MJ/m² | 1 800 m² | 2 700 m² | unlimited | 10 000 m² |

- **No dwelling-specific exemption** exists. Dwellings fall under Table 1 like
  everything else.
- Buildings not placed in a fire class under §11-3 Table 1 need no sectioning
  (no. 4) — that is the risk-class-1 branch, not the dwelling branch.
- At **18 m²** gross, MiniHouse is two orders of magnitude below every threshold.
  Sectioning is not a design driver. The only way §11-7 bites is through the
  §11-6(3) area limit when the cabin stands closer than 8.0 m to another building —
  and even then 18 m² is far below 800 m².

## §11-8 — Brannceller (fire cells)

- A **boenhet is always its own fire cell** (pre-accepted performance, til første
  ledd, no. 7). There is **no area-based exemption for dwellings**; the only small-area
  exceptions in that list concern garages (≤ 50 m² in a single-family house, same
  bruksenhet).
- A *hybelleilighet* with all necessary functions counts as its own boenhet.
- Technical rooms serving **several** other fire cells must be separate cells.
- Table 1, BKL 1: general fire-cell-dividing construction **EI 30** [B 30].
- Doors in fire-cell-dividing walls: normally same resistance as the construction,
  class **Sa**; doors in/to escape routes may be **EI₂ 30-Sa** unless Table 2 says
  otherwise. A door from a boenhet to stairwell Tr 1 need not be self-closing.

**Consequence for MiniHouse:** as a single boenhet with no adjoining unit, its
"fire cell" boundary is its own external envelope, and there is no second cell to
separate from. The internal technical cupboard is **not** automatically a separate
fire cell, because it does not serve several cells; §11-8(1) still requires
different-risk areas to be separated *unless other measures give equivalent
safety*. Whether a small technical cupboard needs EI 30 separation is an
interpretation to confirm, not to assume. If the cabin stands **< 8.0 m** from
another building, §11-6 imposes the separation instead, and then EI 30 becomes real.

## §11-12 — Detection

- Main rule (2)(a): buildings for activity in **risk classes 2–6 must have a fire
  alarm system**. Table 3 gives **brannalarmkategori 1** for risk class 4 in one
  storey (optical smoke detectors in escape routes and common areas); category 2
  from two storeys.
- **Exception (2)(b):** «I byggverk beregnet for få personer og byggverk av mindre
  størrelse kan det brukes røykvarslere dersom rømningsforholdene er særlig enkle
  og oversiktlige.» Smoke alarms must be **mains-connected with battery backup**,
  **interconnected** where a cell needs several, and **battery-only is allowed only
  where the building has no power supply**.
- Pre-accepted performance no. 3 spells out the one-unit dwelling case:
  *Eneboliger, to- til firemannsboliger, rekkehus, kjedehus og fritidsbolig med én
  boenhet i risikoklasse 4* → smoke alarms must cover **kitchen, living room, the
  zone outside bedrooms and technical rooms**; **at least one per storey**; placed so
  the alarm is **at least 60 dB** in habitable rooms and bedrooms **with intervening
  doors closed**.
- Product basis: **NS-EN 14604:2005**, or a detector to **NS-EN 54-7:2018** with a
  sounder to NS-EN 14604:2005. FG-approved dwelling alarm systems with smoke
  detection satisfy the mains-connected requirement.

**Consequence:** MiniHouse does **not** need a full NS 3960 fire alarm system. It
needs interconnected mains-powered smoke alarms with battery backup, covering four
named zones, at ≥60 dB. That is a cheap, buildable requirement — and it is
verifiable on a drawing.

## §11-13 — Exit from the fire cell

- (1) At least **one exit to a safe place**, or exits to **two independent escape
  routes**, or one exit to an escape route with **two alternative directions**.
- (2) Risk class 4 up to **8 storeys** may have an exit to **one** stairwell used as
  an escape route, provided **each dwelling unit has at least one window or balcony
  accessible to rescue and firefighting** (§11-17).
- (4) **The single-direction allowance:** «I lave byggverk beregnet for virksomhet i
  risikoklasse 1, 2, 3 og 4 kan utgangen fra branncelle enten føre til sikkert sted,
  eller til rømningsvei som bare har én rømningsretning, forutsatt at hver branncelle
  har vinduer som er utformet og tilrettelagt for sikker rømning.»

### Escape-window dimensions (the checkable part)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum height | **0.6 m** |
| Minimum width | **0.5 m** |
| Height + width | **≥ 1.5 m** |
| Floor to underside of opening | **≤ 1.0 m**, unless measures ease escape |
| Sloped roof surfaces | **normally not suitable** as escape windows |
| Sill above graded ground, risk classes 1/2/4 | **≤ 5.0 m**; **≤ 7.5 m** with a fixed ladder with back hoops; ladder **≥ 2.0 m** from the window or shielded |
| Operation | easy to open **without special tools**, hinged for easy exit |
| Marking | required, **except in dwelling units** |
| Fire-service access | one accessible escape window is sufficient in a dwelling unit |
| Balcony | equivalent to an escape window when the associated escape measures are met |

Turn/tilt windows with a pivot axis must give an **equivalent effective opening**.

**This is where the current R0 aperture table fails.** Applying the rule to
`fase1/tabla-aberturas.csv`:

| ID | Size | Sill | Height+width | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 door | 900 × 2050 | 0 | – | exit, not an escape window |
| V-01 | 1200 × 1100 | 900 | 2300 mm | **qualifies** — all three dimensions and the 1.0 m sill rule |
| V-02 | 1000 × 900 | 1100 | 1900 mm | dimensions pass, **sill 1100 mm fails** the ≤1.0 m rule |
| V-03 | 500 × 500 | 1500 | 1000 mm | **fails on three counts** — height 500 < 600, sum 1000 < 1500, sill 1500 > 1000 |

So the cabin currently has **one** fully qualifying escape window, V-01, and the
single-direction allowance in §11-13(4) speaks of «vinduer» in the plural for each
fire cell. Whether one qualifying window satisfies a one-room cabin is a
municipal/fire-engineer confirmation, not something to assume. Either way, V-02
and V-03 cannot be counted, and the aperture table has to be revised or the
requirement explicitly justified. `scripts/fire_check.py` reproduces this screen.

### Travel distance (Table 1)

Table 1 as retrieved lists risk classes **1 and 2 → 50 m**, **3 and 5 → 30 m**,
**6 → 25 m**. **Risk class 4 is not separately listed in the retrieved table.**
This is recorded as a source gap rather than filled by inference. It is moot by
geometry for an 18 m² cabin — the longest internal travel distance is a few metres —
but it must not be quoted as a verified number.

## §11-14 — Escape route

- Minimum free width: risk classes **1, 2 and 4 → 0.86 m**; risk classes 3, 5 and
  6 → 1.16 m. Plus **1 cm per person**, whichever is greater.
- **No narrowing** permitted; handrails may protrude up to **10 cm** without
  increasing the free width.
- Maximum distance from the door of a fire cell to the nearest stair or exit where
  **one stair is sufficient: 15 m**; with converging direction **15 m**; with several
  stairs or exits **30 m**.
- Corridors longer than **30 m** must be divided at **≤ 30 m** spacing.
- No numeric minimum height is given in §11-14; the regulation requires
  «tilstrekkelig bredde og høyde».

**Consequence:** the 0.86 m free width is the number that constrains the internal
layout and the door leaf, not the 15 m distance. A 900 mm door leaf gives roughly
0.86 m free width only with the right frame and hinge geometry — worth checking on
the drawing rather than assuming.

## §11-16 — Manual extinguishing

- Risk classes **1, 2 and 4**: **either a hand-held extinguisher or a suitable fire
  hose that reaches into all rooms.**
- Risk classes 3, 5 and 6: fire hose where pressurised water is available, otherwise
  extinguishers.
- Extinguisher sizes: powder **≥ 6 kg ABC**; foam or water **≥ 9 litres**; or
  **≥ 6 litres with efficiency class ≥ 21A** per NS-EN 3-7.
- **Dwelling rule:** «I bolig kan det benyttes formstabil brannslange med innvendig
  diameter på minimum **10 mm**.» Reference standard NS-EN 671-1:2012.
- Hose **≤ 30 m** at full extension; hose cabinets **must not be placed in
  stairwells**; equipment must cover all rooms.
- Marking: photoluminescent or emergency-lit, **except** where the equipment serves
  only one dwelling unit and occupants are expected to know where it is.

**Consequence:** one extinguisher or one ≥10 mm form-stable hose satisfies
MiniHouse. The marking exemption applies.

## §11-17 — Fire-service access and water

- Driving access to the **main entrance and the fire service attack route**. **For
  small buildings in risk class 4 and BKL 1 a distance of up to 50 m is accepted.**
- Top floor **≤ 23 m** above the lowest point of the staging area.
- Outdoor extinguishing water: **≥ 1200 l/min in small-house areas**; **≥ 3000 l/min
  in at least two outlets** elsewhere.
- **Brannkum or hydrant within 25–50 m** of the entrance to the main attack route;
  enough of them to cover all parts of the building.
- Hose lay **≤ 50 m** to reach any part of a floor.
- **Orientation plan** required for risk classes 3, 5 and 6 and larger risk class 2
  buildings — **not for risk class 4**. MiniHouse does not need one.
- Universal-key rule applies above **50 rooms** — not applicable.

**Consequence:** the 50 m access exception and the 1200 l/min small-house capacity
are the two site questions. In a dispersed Bergen plot, the 50 m and the hydrant
distance may be harder to satisfy than anything about the cabin.

## §11-9 — Materials and products (retrieved 2026-11-17)

Table 1A, **brannklasse 1**, covers risk classes 1–5 and therefore applies to MiniHouse:

| Overflater og kledninger | BKL 1 |
|---|---|
| Walls and ceiling/roof, fire cell **up to 200 m²** | **D-s2,d0** [In 2] |
| Walls and ceiling/roof, fire cell **over 200 m²** | D-s2,d0 [In 2] |
| Surfaces in shafts and cavities | B-s1,d0 [In 1] |
| Walls and ceiling/roof **in a fire cell that is an escape route** | **B-s1,d0** [In 1] |
| Floors in a fire cell that is an escape route | Dfl-s1 [G] |
| External cladding surface | **D-s3,d0** [Ut 2] |
| Cladding, fire cell ≤200 m², not an escape route | **K210 D-s2,d0** [K2] |
| Cladding, fire cell >200 m², not an escape route | K210 D-s2,d0 [K2] |
| Cladding in a fire cell that is an escape route | K210 B-s1,d0 [K1] |
| Cladding in shafts and cavities | K210 B-s1,d0 [K1] |

### The good news for reclaimed timber

- A fire cell **up to 200 m²** only needs **D-s2,d0** on walls and ceiling in BKL 1. Timber normally reaches D-s2,d0 with a documented product, so an exposed reclaimed-timber interior is **not excluded** by the surface-class table.
- External cladding needs only **D-s3,d0 [Ut 2]**, and pre-accepted performance E no. 4 allows **BKL 1 and dwellings up to three storeys to have unclassified surfaces in cavities** — which covers the ventilated rainscreen cavity.
- Roof covering must meet **BROOF(t2) [Ta]**. Clay, concrete, slate and **metal sheets are assumed to meet it without further documentation**, so the dark standing-seam metal roof in the reference images is fine. For **småhus** the roof covering may even be unclassified where the distance between buildings is **at least 8 m** — the same 8.0 m that §11-6 already requires.

### The constraint that matters — insulation

- Main rule (§11-9 D): **insulation must meet A2-s1,d0** unless one of nos. 2–9 applies.
- No. 2 allows sandwich elements at **B-s1,d0** in risk classes 1–4, BKL 1.
- No. 9 allows **cellulose or textile-fibre** insulation in **BKL 1 and dwellings up to three storeys**, at Euroclass E or NT Fire 035.
- No. 7 permits combustible insulation in insulated roofs only with a load-bearing substrate meeting **A2-s1,d0** with documented R-class per §11-4, protection below (or A2-s1,d0 insulation of sufficient thickness), and 30 mm A2-s1,d0 above or subdivision into areas up to 400 m².

**A timber rafter does not meet A2-s1,d0**, so no. 7 does not open the door to a timber-rafter roof with wood-fibre insulation. Combined with §11-4(3) no. 6 — where both 6.2 and 6.3 demand A2-s1,d0 insulation — the roof insulation converges on **mineral wool** unless a documented fire-engineering analysis is produced. Two independent clauses point the same way, which is a strong signal rather than a coincidence.

### One interpretation to confirm

Table 1A separates "fire cells that are not an escape route" (D-s2,d0) from "fire cells that are an escape route" (B-s1,d0). For a single-unit cabin whose only exit leads **directly to a safe place** under §11-13(1), there is no separate internal escape route, so the ≤200 m² D-s2,d0 row should govern the interior. That is the expected reading and it is recorded as an interpretation, not a fact: if the municipality treats the whole cabin as an escape route, interior surfaces rise to **B-s1,d0**, which is materially harder for reclaimed timber. Ask; do not assume the favourable reading.

## §11-10 — Technical installations (retrieved 2026-11-17)

- Installations must not significantly increase the risk of fire starting or of fire and smoke spreading. Installations expected to function during a fire must keep that function for the required time, including water, power and signal supply.
- **Ducts, cables and other installations through fire-cell-dividing constructions must not weaken their fire resistance**, documented by test or calculation. Sectioning walls should preferably not be penetrated at all.
- Ventilation: a duct through a fire-separating part must maintain that part's resistance; suspension must maintain the function time; **cooker-hood extract must run in its own duct** with a grease filter and be cleanable along its full length.
- Duct material must be **A2-s1,d0** over the full cross-section. **Exception for småhus:** class **E** ducts and **spirally folded aluminium flexible duct** are permitted, with flexible duct allowed at the cooker-hood connection.
- **Kitchen extract from a dwelling unit: EI 15 A2-s1,d0** if not in a shaft. Commercial kitchens and deep-fat fryers: **EI 30 A2-s1,d0**.
- Pipe penetrations need documented resistance. Plastic pipes **≤32 mm** may pass masonry/cast constructions up to EI 90 and insulated lightweight walls up to EI 60 with a classified sealant. Cast iron **≤110 mm** through EI 60 construction of **≥180 mm** thickness, with **≥250 mm** to combustible material.
- Cables must not run above suspended ceilings or in escape-route cavities unless the cavity holds **< approx. 50 MJ per running metre**, the cables sit in a separate shaft, the ceiling has fire-cell resistance, or the cavity is sprinklered.
- Power for installations that must work during fire: sprinkler protection, cast-in pipes with **≥30 mm** cover, or cables holding function for **≥30 minutes in fire class 1** (≥60 minutes in classes 2 and 3).

**Consequence for MiniHouse:** the kitchen extract needs its own A2-s1,d0 duct with a grease filter, and EI 15 A2-s1,d0 if it is not in a shaft. Any duct or cable crossing the envelope must not silently downgrade it. The 30-minute cable rule only bites if something must operate during a fire — with smoke alarms rather than a powered alarm system that list is short, but the extract fan and any powered damper should be checked against it.

## What this chapter does not settle

- Whether the roof is part of the main load-bearing system (no. 4 R 15) or a
  roof-only construction eligible for no. 6.
- Whether one qualifying escape window satisfies §11-13(4) for a one-room cabin.
- Whether a small technical cupboard needs EI 30 separation.
- The §11-13 Table 1 travel distance for risk class 4, which the retrieved table
  does not list.
- Whether the whole cabin is treated as an escape route for surface classes
  (D-s2,d0 vs B-s1,d0).
- Whether any wood-fibre insulation can be justified in the roof under §11-9 D no. 7
  or by documented analysis.

## Limits

- All values are requirements or pre-accepted performances as published on the
  official pages; they are not results for MiniHouse.
- Nothing here is a fire-engineering analysis, a fire strategy document or a
  sign-off. The R 15 route is the *pre-accepted* route; using it still requires the
  responsible designer to document that the pre-accepted performances are relevant
  and sufficient for this building.
- Surface and insulation classes are requirements on **products**. No reclaimed-timber
  or insulation product has been tested or documented for MiniHouse yet.
- If C-01 lands on *arbeidsbrakke* or *skur*, this whole card stops applying and
  risk class 1 rules govern instead.
